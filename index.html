关于我About me

我是北京大学数学科学学院统计系 2024 级博士研究生，主要研究兴趣为随机矩阵、概率论与高维统计。I am a PhD student in the Department of Statistics, School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, where I began my doctoral studies in 2024.

我的导师是北京大学张志华教授。I am advised by Professor Zhihua Zhang at Peking University.

我的研究关注高维随机模型中的谱性质与普适性，以及马尔可夫链的谱隙、Poincaré 不等式和离散空间上的概率问题。My research interests lie in random matrices, probability, and high-dimensional statistics, with a focus on spectral properties and universality, spectral gaps and Poincaré inequalities, and probability on discrete spaces.
