关于我About me

我是北京大学数学科学学院统计系 2024 级博士研究生，主要研究兴趣为随机矩阵、概率论与高维统计。I am a PhD student in the Department of Statistics, School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, where I began my doctoral studies in 2024.

我的导师是北京大学张志华教授。I am advised by Professor Zhihua Zhang at Peking University.

我的研究兴趣包括随机矩阵与高维概率，尤其关注强收敛与普适性、尖峰张量模型及迭代算法，以及马尔可夫链谱隙与 Poincaré 不等式。My research interests include random matrices and high-dimensional probability, with a focus on strong convergence and universality, spiked tensor models and iterative algorithms, and spectral gaps and Poincaré inequalities for Markov chains.
